# The centre screen — the delegated choice, and the reason

The captain delegated this rather than leaving it open:

> *"Based on what the agent feels works better, have them choose the direction
> for the centre screen. I remember my rationale for both, but things have
> diverged since."*

His instruction is that whoever builds it chooses **and records the reason**.
This is that record. It is a build decision made under delegation, not a captain
decision, and it is `[provisional]` like everything else.

---

## The choice

**Both heroes — but as ONE shared measure with two markers on it, not as two
readouts side by side.** The object on screen is the **gap between them**; the
two absolute numbers are subordinate.

Built in [`whole-a-reclaim.html`](whole-a-reclaim.html) as `drawTrack()`: a
single vertical scale, a marker for each hero, and the distance between the two
markers filled with hatching. There is deliberately **no finish line**, because
the power threshold is parked by his own scope instruction — the scale has no top.

## Why, against the material recorded for the chooser

**1. A race is a relative quantity, and only a shared measure shows it.**
The loss condition is the enemy hero reaching a power threshold, and his own
framing of the whole game is *"preventing your opponent from maximizing that
power journey while maximizing your own."* What a player acts on is not *"his
power is 47"* — it is *"he is ahead and pulling away."* Two absolute readouts
make the player do the subtraction every time they look. One shared measure
renders the subtraction as a distance, which is the cheapest possible read on a
phone glanced at during a fight.

**2. It does not re-adopt the shape he objected to.** The 2026-08-07 objection
was specifically to *"a paired readout beside your own."* That is two mirrored
status blocks. A single scale carrying two markers is a different object: there
are not two readouts, there is one instrument. So this choice **honours the
earlier objection** rather than overruling it — which matters, because the
record explicitly flags the two framings as close enough to the rejected shape
that they must not be smoothed over.

**3. Map control became economic denial, which is a rate effect, and a rate is
only visible as a change in the gap.** Pushing past their front line makes their
farming dangerous, so *"they're going to be earning less gold."* That harm never
shows up as an event — it shows up as the gap widening more slowly than it was.
A shared measure makes that legible. Two absolute numbers hide it.

**4. The minimap already answers position, so the centre screen is free to be
purely about progress.** A minimap showing both heroes at all times is on
record, and it is built here (top-right of the board viewport). That leaves the
centre screen with one job, and doing one job well is what lets it be an
instrument rather than a HUD.

## What this choice cost, stated rather than hidden

An absolute scale would let a player answer *"how close am I to the threshold?"*
This one cannot, because **it has no top**. That is not a defect of the choice —
the threshold is parked, so an absolute scale would have to invent its own
maximum, and inventing one would quietly answer the exact question his scope
instruction parked. **When the threshold is decided, the top of this scale is
where it goes**, and the gap reading survives unchanged.

## What was NOT decided here

- **The right panel's fixed lever.** The build uses his **accepted panel-swapping
  principle** instead: the right panel carries the jungle pre-commitment readout
  by default and swaps to the event panel during a telegraph. That exercises the
  principle he accepted; it does **not** choose which lever owns the slot, and it
  should not be read as choosing one.
- **The leash readout.** The recorded requirement — the player must see at a
  glance where the hero may go — is answered **on the board, not in the bar**:
  reach is legible from the ground itself. Nothing in the bar competes for it.
  A front-line scale in the command bar was considered and **deliberately not
  built**, because a bar that stands in for the front line is the twice-rejected
  abstraction and this is exactly the door it would come back through.
- **The power formula.** `gold/10 + items*8 + camps*2.2` is crude scaffolding so
  the instrument has something to move, labelled as such. It is not a proposal.
