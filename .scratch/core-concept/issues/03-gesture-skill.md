# Gesture as skill expression

Type: prototype
Status: open — **existence challenged**
Blocked by: 01, 02

## ⚠ OPEN CHALLENGE — this ticket may not survive (2026-07-21)

**The user is considering omitting skill shots entirely.** Filed loudly at the
user's explicit instruction — *"so yes do not file it quietly."*

> *"If the cards can affect the jungle and the cards can affect the lane and the
> cards can affect your hero, then we don't necessarily need to add in the user
> having to skill shot something there."*

and, when asked directly:

> *"I'm thinking about omitting it entirely because of the added layer of
> complexity it'll already add on top of everything else."*

**What this challenges.** A locked core-loop constraint: *accrue → combine →
**gesture to cast** (skill-based execution, not tap-and-resolve) → aim into a
lane.* If cards simply act on a chosen target — jungle, lane, or hero — then
casting is **selection, not performance**, and this ticket has no subject.

**Status: not decided.** The constraint stands until the user reverses it
explicitly. But this ticket should not be worked as though its premise were safe,
and the question below ("what makes it skillful rather than ceremonial") is now
sharper than when it was written — the honest answer may be *nothing, so cut it.*

**What omitting it would cost.** The gesture is currently the only proposed home
for execution skill. Without it, player skill lives entirely in selection,
timing, and deckbuilding — a more accessible game, and a flatter one. That trade
is real in both directions and belongs to 04 and 18.

## Question

What is the gesture, and what exactly makes it *skillful* rather than ceremonial?

The user was explicit: casting should not be "click and it works instantly."
The thumb gesture is meant to be where execution skill lives. But a gesture that
merely confirms an already-decided outcome is decoration with extra steps.

The answer must settle:

- **What the gesture controls.** Direction? Trajectory? Timing? Power via
  distance or hold duration? Path accuracy against a target shape?
- **What failure looks like.** Can you miss? Partially miss? Is a bad gesture a
  weaker spell, a mis-aimed spell, or a wasted cast? Failure has to sting enough
  to matter and not so much that it feels arbitrary on a touchscreen.
- **Thumb ergonomics.** One-handed play is the realistic mobile posture. A
  gesture requiring precision at the top of the screen fails that.
- **Accessibility.** A purely dexterity-gated cast excludes players. Is there an
  assist mode, and does it cost anything competitively?
- **Readability to the opponent.** Does your gesture telegraph anything?

Prototype this on a real device. Gesture feel cannot be evaluated on paper.

Blocked until the board shape (01) and the thing being cast (02) exist.
