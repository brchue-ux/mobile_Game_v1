# Unlock progression & the hook

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 02

## Question

If every card is obtainable by everyone and nothing is purchase-exclusive, what
makes unlocking them compelling?

The user was precise about the constraint and equally clear that the motivation
problem is unsolved: *"shared full pool in the sense that everyone could get all
the same cards. not full shared as in all cards unlocked day 1. hook will need to
be created here somehow."*

Removing the chase mechanic that CCGs normally rely on leaves a real hole. This
ticket fills it.

The answer must settle:

- **What drives unlocking.** Play time? Mastery milestones? Challenges? Choice —
  pick your next unlock rather than receive a random one?
- **Where the "hook" comes from** once artificial scarcity is off the table.
  Candidates: mastery depth, identity/expression, discovery of combos,
  competitive ladder, social.
- **Whether the unlock pace is a fairness problem.** A player three weeks in has
  more combos available than a day-one player. Does PvP account for this, and
  how? (Relates to fog: matchmaking fairness.)
- **Whether unlocking is even the right frame.** If combos are the real depth,
  maybe every card is available immediately and progression lives entirely
  elsewhere. Worth putting on trial rather than assuming.

Blocks 07 (monetization) — you cannot decide what's sellable before knowing what
progression exists.
