# Banking — combining over time

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 02

## Question

What is the banking mechanic, and what does a merge produce?

Split out of 02. The user's instinct, by analogy to Team Fight Tactics: instead
of casting a card now, you can **bank** it; banked cards merge into something
better. The cost is tempo — a banked card is not available for the lane fight
happening right now.

Why this is promising: the moment-to-moment decision is a **binary made
repeatedly under pressure** — bank or cast. Near-zero cognitive load per
instance, real strategic depth in aggregate. That shape fits the accessibility
priority (02) better than anything else surfaced so far.

The answer must settle:

- **What a merge produces.** Four candidates, all wanted by the user eventually:
  1. **Jungle / map plays** — persistent effects in the jungle rather than lane
     damage. Would give banking a purpose distinct from raw power *and* give the
     jungle a reason to exist (see 12).
  2. **Higher-tier version of the same card** — TFT-literal. Legible and cheap;
     pure power scaling, no new decisions.
  3. **Transmutation into a different card** — strong discovery hook, but
     smuggles recipe memorization back in after 02 ruled it out.
  4. **Charge toward an ultimate** — simplest; makes *which* card you bank stop
     mattering.

  Assessment from 02: pick **one** for the vertical slice. All four is four
  subsystems taught under a running clock — the trade accessibility refuses.
- **How many cards a bank holds**, and whether there are multiple banks.
- **What the risk actually is.** The user said "you risk putting a card in a
  bank." Is the only cost tempo, or can a bank be lost, stolen, or attacked?
- **Whether banking is visible to the opponent.** Interacts with 10.
- **Whether merges are automatic on threshold or player-triggered.**
