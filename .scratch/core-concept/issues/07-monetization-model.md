# Monetization model

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 06

## Question

What is actually sold, and what keeps it from being resented?

The constraint from the user: microtransactions should be "very light" and "not
really perceived as a hated microtransaction game." Cards are already ruled out
as purchase-exclusive, which removes the genre's default revenue engine.

The answer must settle:

- **What's on sale.** Cosmetics, spell VFX, board themes, a battle pass,
  convenience, expression items?
- **Whether unlock acceleration is sellable.** This is the slippery slope — it is
  technically not pay-to-win, and players frequently resent it anyway. Decide it
  deliberately.
- **What "light" means numerically.** Is there a revenue target this has to
  clear, or is this a passion project where the answer can be "barely anything"?
  These produce very different designs and the difference should be explicit.
- **The resentment test.** For each item sold, state why a player who *didn't*
  buy it doesn't feel worse off. Anything failing that test doesn't ship.
- **Games to borrow from.** Dota 2's model was cited approvingly during charting;
  worth examining what actually transfers to a game with no hero roster.

Blocked until 06 establishes what progression exists to monetize around.
